# 🚀 Stellar Strategy

**One Island. One Launch. One Chance.**

Stellar Strategy is a story-driven space game for kids, built by **MEC_Exelion_Squad** from Mymensingh Engineering College for the **NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026**.

## About

Space and engineering can feel out of reach for children. Stellar Strategy turns them into hands-on challenges where every decision has a consequence. Players manage tight budgets, make engineering trade-offs, and learn how real missions succeed or fail.

## The Story

The Isle of Solana is a small island nation in the middle of the ocean. While countries like America, Russia and Japan push ahead in space, Solana decides it won't be left behind and creates its own space agency.

You are the **Director General**. Your first job is to prove the agency can reach a launch.

- **Succeed:** more missions follow, and other countries step in to support Solana.
- **Fail:** the space program is shut down and everyone who joined loses their job.

## Missions

| # | Mission | What you do |
|---|---------|-------------|
| 01 | **The Kármán Breach** | Design a rocket on a tiny national budget and cross the Kármán Line in one attempt. |
| 02 | **The Storm Sentinel** | Launch Solana's first weather satellite, inspired by NASA's TIROS-1, and warn your people of storms. |
| 03 | **Project Red Horizon** | Send a rover to Jezero Crater: choose your transfer, wheels and instruments, then survive a solar storm and the seven minutes of Mars entry. |

Missions unlock in order. Log in to save your progress.
